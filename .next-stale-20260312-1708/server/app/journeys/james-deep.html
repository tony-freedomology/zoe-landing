James writes to a scattered church — people who claimed faith but lived differently from Monday to Saturday. His letter is less a theology lecture and more a direct conversation: you say you believe. Does your life show it?

Start This Journey

Duration

Check-ins

10 readings

Passages

This 10-day journey moves through the entire Book of James, one passage per day. Each morning, Zoe delivers the passage with historical context and a question to prime your reading.

Then the conversation unfolds — Zoe engages with what stood out to you, asks follow-up questions that go deeper, and helps you land on one concrete thing to carry into your day. By day 10, you'll have wrestled with every chapter of James and built a thread of personal reflection that connects across the whole book.

The 10 Days

1
James 1:1–4·Trials as Teachers

“What challenge in your life right now might actually be producing something good in you?”

2
James 1:5–8·Asking for Wisdom

“Where do you most need wisdom today — and are you actually asking for it?”

3
James 1:12–18·The Source of Temptation

“What desire, if left unchecked, tends to lead you somewhere you don't want to go?”

4
James 1:19–27·Hearing and Doing

“Is there a truth you know but haven't acted on yet? What's stopping you?”

5
James 2:1–13·No Favoritism

“Who in your life do you treat as less important because of status, appearance, or wealth?”

6
James 2:14–26·Faith and Works

“What would someone know about your faith just from watching your actions this week?”

7
James 3:1–12·Taming the Tongue

“What's something you said recently that you wish you could take back?”

8
James 3:13–4:3·Two Kinds of Wisdom

“Is the wisdom guiding your decisions right now from God — or from somewhere else?”

9
James 4:4–17·Humility Before God

“Where are you striving for something God hasn't given you? What would it look like to submit that?”

10
James 5:7–20·Patient Endurance

“Who in your life needs someone to turn them back toward God right now?”
