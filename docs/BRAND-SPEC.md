# Zoe Brand Spec

This document is the working source of truth for Zoe brand direction across marketing pages, journeys, imagery, UI styling, and copy.

## 1. Brand Summary

**One-line brand definition**

Zoe is a quiet, spiritually grounded tool that helps people walk with Jesus throughout the ordinary hours of the day.

**Core promise**

Zoe helps people move from fragmented good intentions to an integrated life with God.

**Visual system name**

`The Modern Apothecary (Bold Edition)`

**Creative north star**

`The Living Editorial`

## 2. Brand Positioning

### What Zoe is

- A day-long discipleship tool
- An SMS-first experience with almost no learning curve
- A tool that helps people pay attention to God, Scripture, and real life
- A product that points outward to church, community, and faithful action

### What Zoe is not

- Not an AI pastor
- Not a replacement for the Holy Spirit
- Not a substitute for church, pastoral care, or Christian community
- Not a generic chatbot with Bible verses
- Not a productivity app wearing spiritual language

## 3. Emotional Outcome

Every Zoe touchpoint should leave people feeling:

- more grounded
- more attentive
- more hopeful
- less fragmented
- more willing to take a faithful next step

The tone should never make users feel managed, optimized, or spiritually marketed to.

## 4. Voice & Copy Style

### Voice attributes

- quiet
- direct
- warm
- literate
- pastoral without being preachy
- confident without sounding salesy

### Copy rules

- Prefer short declarative lines over inflated marketing phrasing
- Use concrete spiritual language instead of abstract self-help language
- Make room for reflection; do not over-explain every idea
- Write like a thoughtful guide, not a hype brand
- Let the strongest claims come from clarity, not intensity

### Avoid language like

- Revolutionary
- Optimize your spiritual life
- AI pastor
- Faith operating system
- Hyper-personalized engagement engine
- Frictionless discipleship platform
- Productivity-first spiritual framing

## 5. Visual System

There is one canonical default Zoe brand identity.

Do not use older prototype directions as the source of truth for new default-brand work. `jesus-red` may remain as a campaign-specific surface, but it is not the default brand system.

### Core feeling

Zoe should feel:
- calm
- hopeful
- premium
- restrained
- trustworthy
- warm
- modern
- spiritually grounded
- light

It should not feel:
- corporate
- glossy
- dark and dramatic
- glassmorphic
- SaaS-blue
- editorial-magazine-like
- institutionally green

### Core palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oat | `#FCF9F4` | Default page background |
| Surface | `#F6F3EE` | Tonal layering and secondary sections |
| Card White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, forms, elevated content |
| Ink | `#1C1C19` | Primary text |
| Muted Ink | `#5F5E5B` | Supporting text |
| Outline | `#BBCAC1` | Quiet structure and dividers |
| Jade | `#1DC286` | Canonical Zoe green, main CTA and primary accent |
| Leaf Green | `#1DC286` | Alias to Jade for supporting active accents |
| Forest | `#007354` | Sparse trust-oriented deeper accent |

### Color behavior

- Jade is the primary action color
- Primary CTA text on Jade is always white
- Forest is sparse and supporting, not a main slab background
- Prefer tonal surface shifts over high-contrast section bands
- Avoid navy, cyan, muddy emerald, or glossy gradient logic

### Typography

- **Primary system:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`
- **Accent serif:** `Newsreader Italic`

#### Type behavior

- Main hero headlines stay sans-serif
- Section headlines stay sans-serif
- Buttons, nav, forms, metadata, and body stay sans-serif
- Serif appears only as a rare trust-bearing accent

Good serif uses:
- a short trust line
- a reflective aside
- a privacy/trust micro-heading
- a small brand-level phrase

Bad serif uses:
- hero headline
- nav
- long body copy
- feature-card titles
- CTA labels

### Composition rules

- favor calm, balanced layouts over editorial asymmetry
- use generous whitespace
- use large, premium radii
- rely on tonal layering and subtle shadows
- avoid glassmorphism and visible heavy shadowing

## 6. Page Guidance

### Home page

Preserve these structurally:
- hero section
- sticky SMS section
- sticky rhythms environment and imagery

Focus unification work on:
- waitlist / opt-in
- objection handling / thesis / trust beats
- FAQ
- supporting CTA sections

### About page

Should feel warm, trustworthy, thoughtful, and light. Avoid dark slab closes.

### Blog and guides

Should feel quiet, readable, premium, and lightly editorial only in restraint, not in typography dominance.

### Features page

Should feel clear, calm, product-confident, and warm. Use tonal card layering instead of harsh contrast.

### Journey marketing pages

Should feel grounded, premium, and spiritually serious without drifting into generic SaaS styling.

### Church-facing pages

Should still feel warm and modern, not institutional or enterprise-admin.

## 7. Interaction Principles

- Favor stillness over spectacle
- Keep motion gentle
- CTA moments should feel invitational, not pushy
- Forms and accordions should feel minimal and paper-like
- Trust and privacy language should be concrete and visible

## 8. Non-Negotiables

1. Default Zoe is a light brand.
2. No gradients or glassmorphism in core default-brand surfaces.
3. Plus Jakarta Sans is the dominant typeface.
4. Newsreader Italic is rare and trust-bearing only.
5. Jade is the primary CTA fill.
6. CTA text on green is white.
7. The interface should feel like warm premium paper, not digital glass.
8. If a page feels too corporate, remove dark slabs, navy support colors, and hard borders.
9. If a page feels too editorial, reduce serif and flourish before changing the structure.
